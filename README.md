## Ecommerce Sales Analysis

## Project Overview
- This project analyzes retail business performance across sales, profit, customers, products, and operations. It aims to identify key drivers of revenue and profitability, understand customer and regional behavior, and evaluate the impact of discounts, returns, and delivery on overall performance. The insights help improve decision-making in pricing, operations, and growth strategy.

## Dataset Source
- Source: Kaggle

## Business Questions
- Which product categories drive the highest revenue and profit, and how diversified is the portfolio?
- How do discounts affect sales, demand and profitability?
- How does delivery performance influence returns and operational efficiency?
- Which regions contribute most to revenue and profit, and where are growth opportunities?
- What payment methods are most preferred, and how do they affect revenue contribution?
- Which customer segments generate the highest lifetime business value?
- Do larger order volumes and discounts lead to sustainable profitability?
- What seasonal demand patterns can inform inventory and marketing decisions?
- What factors are constraining profitability, and where should optimization focus?

## Key Insights
- Business generated $5.87M revenue and $970K profit from 34,500 orders and 7,903 customers, indicates a solid, stable business with consistent performance.
- Electronics leads revenue (56.6%) and profit, mainly because of high-value products and strong demand.
- Home category performs efficiently with strong margins and fewer returns making it highly profitable.
- Grocery is loss-making (-$9.2K), likely due to low margins and higher operational costs.
- Overall return rate is low (5.5%) which indicates good product quality and smooth operations overall.
- Fashion (8.3%) and Electronics (7.3%) have the highest returns likely due to sizing issues and expectation mismatches.
- South region leads revenue, while Central lags, showing uneven regional demand strength.
- Credit Card is the most used payment method, driven by convenience and trust.
- Discounts hurt profitability with limited evidence of demand boost.
- Delivery time (4.8 days) is stable - logistics doesn’t appear to be a major issue.
- Customers aged 45+ are the most valuable, especially in electronics,might be due to higher disposible income.
- Revenue is evenly split between male and female customers, no strong gender bias in buying behavior.
- Bulk orders stay profitable even with discounts.
- Overall performance is stable over time, suggesting a mature and predictable business.
- Peak sales happen in April, October, and December, likely due to festive and seasonal buying patterns.


## Recommendations (What companies can do)
-	Grocery should be fixed or exited as grocery retail operate on very low margins (~1–3%) (McKinsey Retail Insights)
-	Blanket discounts should be reduced as they can lower profitability by 10–30% (Harvard Business Review)
-	More investment should go into Home products, as they typically deliver 20–40% margins (Deloitte Consumer Report),
-	Dependency on Electronics should be reduced due to high price volatility in the category (Statista E-commerce Reports)
-	Retention should focus on 45+ customers who generally show higher AOV and loyalty (Nielsen Consumer Behavior Report)
-	Discounts should be shifted to targeted bulk strategies that can improve profits by 5–15% (Boston Consulting Group)
-	Fashion and Electronics returns should be addressed as e-commerce return rates average 20–30% in Fashion (NRF)
-	Seasonal campaigns should be prioritized in April, October and December as these periods contribute up to 30–40% of annual sales in many markets (Adobe Digital Economy Index)
-	RFM analysis should be implemented next as it can improve retention and targeting by 20–50% (Gartner).

## Conclusion
The business is stable and well-performing, with strong revenue driven by Electronics and efficient profits from Home. However, profitability is limited by heavy discounting, a loss-making Grocery category, and regional imbalance. Future growth depends on improving margins, optimizing weak categories, and using better customer and seasonal targeting.
